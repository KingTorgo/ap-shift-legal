PRIVACY AT A GLANCE

AP Shift stores character sheets, encounter information, dice appearance settings, and roll history on your device. The app does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking.

YOUR DATA

AP Shift does not operate a server that receives or stores your character information. Your data remains in the app's local device storage unless you choose to export a backup or share a character file.

When you use Export Backup or Share, your device's share sheet sends the selected JSON file only to the destination you choose. When you use Restore Backup or Import Character, AP Shift reads only the file you select.
